## Brand & Style

This design system serves individuals with speech disabilities, their caregivers, speech therapists, and conversational partners. The brand ethos departs completely from sterile, clinical medical appliances or infantilizing assistive interfaces. Instead, it positions assistive communication as an exquisite, empowering instrument of human dignity, agency, and presence.

The visual style merges humanistic minimalism with warm tactile craftsmanship:
- **Dignified & Warm:** Replaces utilitarian gray-and-blue medical palettes with radiant warm ivory, light champagne, and artisanal muted gold.
- **Calm & Focused:** Ample negative space relieves cognitive and motor load, ensuring interactions remain unhurried, reassuring, and completely under the user's control.
- **Accessible Sophistication:** High-clarity typography paired with clear tactile interaction affordances preserves effortless accessibility while delivering a bespoke, premium physical-device feel.

## Colors

The color palette centers on radiant, golden-tinted neutrals that feel tactile, gentle on the eyes during prolonged use, and universally welcoming:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** The primary canvas is Warm Ivory (`#FCFAF5`), establishing ambient warmth without the glare of raw white. Secondary grouping backgrounds use Light Champagne (`#F6F0E1`). Active cards and conversational stages rest on pristine White (`#FFFFFF`) to maximize visual contrast and clean delineation.
- **Brand & Action Metals:** Muted Primary Gold (`#C9A44C`) acts as the anchor for primary vocal triggers, generation states, and focused interactions. Deep Gold (`#9C7830`) provides WCAG AAA contrast for focused interactive text, active outlines, and prominent icons. Soft Champagne Gold (`#E8D7A8`) delivers soft interactive fills and ambient highlights.
- **Content & Support:** Deep Charcoal (`#242321`) delivers crisp, high-legibility readability for all core transcriptions and primary titles. Warm Gray (`#77736A`) serves secondary labels, timestamps, and assistive meta-information. Sage Green (`#5E8B68`) acts as an organic, non-alarmist affirmative confirmation for speech playback, saved phrases, and synced states.
- **Structure:** Fine borders use `#E9E2D3` to separate modular panels without visual noise.

## Typography

Typography balances structural elegance and low-vision legibility:

- **Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a welcoming, rounded, contemporary aesthetic that breaks away from cold analytical conventions. It maintains crisp legibility with gentle geometry.
- **Reading & Transcription:** `Atkinson Hyperlegible Next` is dedicated to core communication, predicted speech sentences, rapid-phrase tiles, and conversational histories. Specifically sculpted to distinguish ambiguous glyphs (such as I, l, 1, 0, and O), it minimizes misinterpretations during rapid or stressful communication moments.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Conversational display text must never drop below `body-lg` (`18px`) in interactive AAC panels. Primary speech output prompts utilize `body-xl` (`20px`) with relaxed line heights (`30px`) to remain clearly legible at conversational arm's length.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around a predictable, low-fatigue fluid grid system:

- **Grid System:** 12-column adaptive fluid grid on desktop (1024px+), 8 columns on tablet (768px-1023px), and 4 columns on mobile (<768px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1280px` to prevent excessive scanning distances.
- **Motor Accommodation:** Touch and gaze targets enforce generous spacing rhythm. Core conversational phrase banks maintain minimum component gutters of `space-md` (`1rem`) to prevent accidental adjacent activation for users with fine motor impairments.
- **Breakpoints & Layout:** 
  - *Mobile (<768px):* Single-column conversational stream with fixed bottom-docked quick-access prediction tray. Outer margins set to `margin-sm` (`1rem`).
  - *Tablet (768px–1023px):* Two-pane split view (quick vocabulary bank on left 5 columns, real-time sentence construction and output buffer on right 3 columns).
  - *Desktop (1024px+):* Three-column workspace (category navigation, conversational synthesizer board, dialogue session transcript).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle atmospheric warmth rather than heavy, sterile dropshadows. Depth mimics fine architectural paper sheets and warm tactile keys:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FCFAF5` flat matte surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with a delicate perimeter border (`1px solid #E9E2D3`) and a warm ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(156, 120, 48, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(36, 35, 33, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Panels):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(156, 120, 48, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 35, 33, 0.04)`. Used for dynamic phrase prediction cards and active modals.
- **Level 3 (Overlay / Speech Drawer / Modals):** `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(36, 35, 33, 0.1), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(156, 120, 48, 0.06)`. Accompanied by a gentle warm backdrop blur (`rgba(252, 250, 245, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Active Tactile Depress:** Interactive buttons depress inward on press: shadow collapses to `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(156, 120, 48, 0.15)` with `transform: translateY(1px)` to give distinct, reassuring physical confirmation.

## Shapes

This design system employs roundedness level `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` base), deliberately expanded across major touch surfaces to reflect a comforting, non-clinical aesthetic:

- **Structural Cards:** Rounded cards utilize `20px` to `24px` radius (`rounded-xl` to `1.5rem`), creating smooth, organic resting contours that soothe visual tension.
- **Interactive Controls & Buttons:** Buttons utilize `12px` to `16px` radius (`rounded-lg` through intermediate scale) for a pill-softened rectangular feel that maintains structural click-target geometry.
- **Pills & Quick-Tags:** AI prediction suggestion pills, category tags, and voice pitch tags use full-pill curvature (`9999px`) to distinguish situational quick-insert items from primary structural controls.
- **Focus Rings:** Enforce a double-ring aesthetic: `2px` solid white gap followed by a `3px` ring in Deep Gold (`#9C7830`) with `4px` border radius offset.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary "Speak / Express" Button:** Solid Muted Primary Gold (`#C9A44C`) with Deep Charcoal text (`#242321`) or high-contrast deep tone, minimum height `56px`, horizontal padding `24px`, radius `14px`. Hover transitions to `#BA953E`. Active state applies `1px` downward translation.
- **Secondary / Quick-Phrase Buttons:** Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle border (`1px solid #E9E2D3`) and text in `#242321`. Hover fills with `#F6F0E1`.
- **Destructive / Clear Buffer:** Subtle warm ivory surface with `#77736A` text; active warning tint in muted rust only upon multi-step trigger.

### Conversation Phrase Chips & AI Suggestions
- Encapsulated in soft pill shapes (`radius: 9999px`). Background `#F6F0E1` with border `#E8D7A8`. Typography set to `Atkinson Hyperlegible Next` `label-md`.
- Tap or keypress provides immediate micro-expansion animation with Sage Green check indicator (`#5E8B68`) upon utterance insertion.

### Input Fields & Speech Buffer
- **Speech Synthesis Field:** Expansive, prominent conversational staging card (`#FFFFFF`, radius `20px`, padding `20px`). Minimum height `120px` to accommodate multi-sentence composition. Text rendered in `body-xl` (`#242321`).
- Default input border: `1.5px solid #E9E2D3`; Focus state: `2px solid #C9A44C` with ambient gold halo (`0 0 0 4px rgba(201, 164, 76, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Distinct `24px x 24px` hit targets. Checked fill uses `#C9A44C` with crisp white inner check/glyph. Unchecked border uses `#77736A` (`2px solid`) on `#FFFFFF` to ensure high contrast against light surfaces.

### Cards & Communication Boards
- Enclosed in `#FFFFFF` with `20px` or `24px` corner radiuses, surrounded by `1px solid #E9E2D3`. Internal padding `24px`.
- Dedicated "Quick Need" AAC matrix cards incorporate bold visual symbols paired with high-contrast text labels directly below the glyph.

### Voice Waveform & Status Indicator
- Real-time vocal synthesis is represented by an organic animated audio bar array in Deep Gold (`#9C7830`) and Sage Green (`#5E8B68`), confirming sound projection without jarring audio feedback spikes.
